NDT7 Speed Test — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

NDT7 Speed Test is a network diagnostic application for HarmonyOS wearable devices. It measures Internet connection performance (download/upload throughput, latency, and packet loss) using the open Measurement Lab (M-Lab) NDT7 protocol.

What the app does NOT collect

The app does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, accounts)
  • Contacts, messages, photos, or files on your device
  • Precise GPS location or location history
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers
  • Any data for commercial purposes

What data is involved in a speed test

When you run a speed test, the app connects to M-Lab's open research servers. The following technical data is generated and published to M-Lab's public, open research dataset (BigQuery and Google Cloud Storage):

  • Your public IP address and approximate ISP / autonomous system (AS) network
  • Download and upload throughput measurements
  • TCP diagnostics (round-trip time, jitter, retransmission rate)
  • Test timestamps and duration
  • Anonymous client metadata (client name and version)

M-Lab publishes this data openly and permanently to support global Internet research. Your IP address is included in the published dataset. See the M-Lab Privacy & Data Policy for details.

Consent

The app asks for your explicit consent to the M-Lab Data Policy before the first test. No measurement is performed without consent.

You can withdraw consent at any time via More → Reset Consent in the app, or by uninstalling the app. After withdrawal, no new measurements are performed until you consent again. Previously published M-Lab data cannot be deleted retroactively, as it is released to the public domain under M-Lab's data policy.

Permissions

The app requests only the INTERNET permission, which is required to connect to speed test servers. It requests no sensitive permissions.
